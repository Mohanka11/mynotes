Artificial Intelligence Study Guide

Core Concepts, Branches, Classifications & Practical Machine Learning

1 Artificial Intelligence (AI) Overview

  • Definition: The ability of a machine to think, understand, learn, and work like human beings. It is a computer program capable of learning and making decisions based on data.
  • Core Aim: The study of how a human brain thinks, learns, decides, and works when solving complex problems.
  • Real-World Application: Facial recognition is a primary application, executed via photo scans, video footage, or direct facial scanning.

2 Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning

Machine Learning (ML)

A branch of AI enabling computers to act without explicit programming. It analyzes historical data patterns to predict future output values.

Deep Learning

A specialized subfield of AI inspired by the neural architecture and learning mechanisms of the human brain (powering tasks like facial recognition).

3 Types of Artificial Intelligence

AI is categorized into three stages based on capability:

Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)

Systems engineered and specialized to perform a single dedicated task exceptionally well.

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

A hypothetical machine capable of comprehending, learning, and reasoning about the world on par with human intellect.

Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI)

An advanced form of AI that surpasses human capabilities across virtually all economically valuable work and cognitive tasks.

4 Professional Roles in AI

  • AI Engineers & Scientists: Tackle complex technical problems by implementing traditional software architectures alongside machine learning models.
  • AI Researchers: Specialize in designing experiments, developing novel algorithmic approaches, and analyzing structured data.
  • Robotics Experts: Build and maintain automated robotic systems for enterprise workflows (operating with variable levels of human supervision).
  • Data Scientists: Collect, process, analyze, and build predictive models on massive datasets. They synthesize mathematics, statistics, and computer science to drive organizational strategy.

5 Google Teachable Machine

Definition: A web-based tool developed by Google that enables users to rapidly build custom machine learning models using camera inputs that process visual data similar to human vision.

Step-by-Step Creation Guide:

  1. Open Website: Visit https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/ and click Get Started.
  2. Start Project: Select New Project, choose Image Project, and select Standard image model.
  3. Define Classes: Label your categories (e.g., rename Class 1 to 'Proper Uniform' and Class 2 to 'Improper Uniform').
  4. Feed Data (Upload Images): Provide sample imagery via drag-and-drop or import from Google Drive (a high volume of diverse samples increases accuracy).
  5. Train the Model: Click Train Model to begin processing sample data and compiling the weights.
  6. Preview: Test the live camera feed in the preview panel to observe real-time classification probability bars.
