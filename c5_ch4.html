Google Workspace & Forms Study Guide

Comprehensive Overview, Survey Creation & Analysis

1 Google Workspace Overview

A collection of online tools designed to help users create and share documents easily.

  • Gmail & Meet: Used for communication.
  • Calendar: Keeps track of time and meeting schedules.
  • Drive: Used to store files securely in the cloud.
  • Docs, Sheets & Slides: Used for creating text documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.
  • Forms: Dedicated application for conducting surveys and quizzes.
  • Sites: Used for building websites.

2 Introduction to Google Forms & Surveys

Definition: Google Forms is an online survey software tool used to create surveys, quizzes, and collect information (alternative tools include SurveyMonkey and Zoho). A survey is a method of collecting opinions or data from a group through structured questions.

Advantages of Online Surveys:

  • Can be taken at any time by respondents.
  • Data can be collected from anywhere across the globe.
  • Responses are automatically compiled.
  • Results are easily converted into charts and graphs for analysis.

3 Accessing Google Forms

Navigate to https://docs.google.com/forms/u/0/ while signed in to your Google account and select Blank to create a new form.

Standard Interface Controls:

Customize Theme Preview Undo Redo Send Questions Responses Settings More

4 Creating a Survey & Question Types

Google Forms provides specific field types tailored to the type of response required:

Short Answer

Ideal for brief text entries such as a name, email address, or quick one-line opinion.

Paragraph

Used when respondents need to provide extended, multi-line detailed answers.

Multiple Choice

Objective question format where the form-filler must select exactly one option.

Drop Down

Functions like multiple choice with options hidden in a collapsed list; best for long lists.

Checkboxes

Selected when a question allows the respondent to pick multiple answers at once.

Date

Restricts user entry to a standardized date format (Month/Day/Year).

Linear Scale

Captures opinions or ratings along a customizable numeric scale from 0 to 10.

5 Form Building Controls

  • Making Questions Compulsory: Toggle the Required switch to force the form-filler to provide an answer before submitting.
  • Adding Questions: Click the + icon located on the floating side panel.
  • Adding Options: Click Add option to add extra choice selections to Multiple Choice or Drop-Down fields.
  • Form-filler: The designated term for any user who answers and submits the survey.

6 Previewing and Sharing Forms

  • Preview: Click the Preview (eye) icon in the top toolbar to view the form exactly as respondents will experience it.
  • Sharing Methods (via Send button):
    • E-mail: Send the form directly to individual email addresses.
    • Link Sharing: Copy a URL to distribute via communication channels or social media.
    • HTML Embedding: Copy an <iframe> embed snippet to display the form directly on a website.

7 Collecting and Analyzing Responses

Navigate to the Responses tab at the top of the form editor to review survey data across three views:

  • Summary Tab: Aggregates all submissions into automated real-time visual charts and graphs.
  • Question & Individual Views: Allows examining responses on a per-question basis or viewing each complete submission separately.
  • Google Sheets Integration: Click the View in Sheets icon (green spreadsheet button) to link responses directly into a live, auto-updating Google Sheets workbook.
